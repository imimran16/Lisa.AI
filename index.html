Privacy Policy

How Lisa handles your data

Your privacy matters. Lisa uses what you say only to talk with you.

  • Your text messages are sent to our AI service so that Lisa can write a reply.
  • In Voice Assistant, the microphone is on only while a voice session is running. It stops when you close the voice screen, and you can mute it at any time.
  • Your chat history and Lisa's memories are saved only on your own device (in your browser), not on our servers. You can delete them any time with the bin button in the chat, or Memories → Clear all.
  • To build memories, your recent messages are sent to our AI service. Please do not share passwords, OTPs, bank details or ID numbers with Lisa. Our hosting and AI providers may process requests as needed to deliver the service.
  • We count anonymous usage (visits, messages and voice sessions) using a random ID, never your name or what you wrote, only to see how many people use Lisa. If you send feedback, we store your rating and message.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.
  • Lisa is an AI companion, not a doctor or therapist. If you are in distress or in danger, please contact someone you trust or a local professional or emergency service.
  • Questions about privacy? Contact the creator through the CreatorJourney section.
